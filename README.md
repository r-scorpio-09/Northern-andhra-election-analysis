# Northern Andhra Election Turnout Analysis (2014–2024)

An exploratory data analysis (EDA) of voter turnout and electoral participation across constituencies in Visakhapatnam (VSKP), Vizianagaram (VZM), and Srikakulam (SKLM) districts across the 2014, 2019, and 2024 election cycles.

---

## 📌 Project Overview
This project tracks historical voter counts across Assembly constituencies in the Northern Andhra region of Andhra Pradesh. It highlights long-term participation trends, growth trajectories, and cross-district voter distributions.

### Covered Districts:
- **Visakhapatnam (VSKP):** 15 constituencies (Bhimili, Vskp East/South/North/West, Gajuwaka, Anakapalle, etc.)
- **Vizianagaram (VZM):** 9 constituencies (Bobbili, Cheepurupalli, Gajapathinagaram, Salur, etc.)
- **Srikakulam (SKLM):** 10 constituencies (Tekkali, Srikakulam, Etcherla, Palasa, etc.)

---

## 📊 Key Highlights
- **Consistently High Turnout:** Major urban and rural segments showed steady voter base expansion between 2014 and 2024.
- **District Comparisons:** Analysis reveals significant growth in fast-urbanizing constituencies such as Bhimili and Gajuwaka.
- **Data Export:** Raw tabular data is structured and exported into multi-sheet spreadsheets for downstream analytics.
